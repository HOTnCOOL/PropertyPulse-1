Deposit Policy
Overview: We aim to make your booking process as straightforward as possible. Below are the details of our deposit policy, designed to clarify the requirements for various booking scenarios.

Security Deposit:

No security deposit is charged for fully prepaid bookings.
For all stays longer than 3 days that are only partially paid, a security deposit must be paid in advance or guaranteed by credit card.
The deposit is fully refundable at checkout.
If at least two packs of the same type are prepaid, the deposit can be reduced by half.
Deposit Amount: The amount of the deposit is determined according to the duration of your stay:

$90 for stays longer than 3 days and shorter than 2 weeks (one daily rate deposit).
$420 for stays longer than 2 weeks and shorter than 2 months (one weekly rate deposit).
$1200 for stays longer than 2 months (one monthly rate deposit).
Summary of Payment Options
To help you better understand your payment options, we present several scenarios illustrating how your bookings may be structured based on preferred payment plans:

Monthly Payment:

Two monthly packs and two weekly packs with regular prices apply for a booking duration of 2.5 months. The total will be calculated without any upfront prepayment discounts.
Initial Payment Required: First monthly pack + deposit.
Weekly Payment:

Paying in weekly installments allows flexibility; however, the total amount at the end of your stay tends to be higher than the first monthly scenario.
Initial Payment Required: First weekly pack + deposit.
Daily Payment:

Users can opt to pay on a daily basis, offering maximum flexibility in payment scheduling.
Initial Payment Required: First daily pack + deposit.
Visual Breakdown of Charges
Each payment option is accompanied by an estimation displaying the total accommodation cost at the bottom of each respective package, highlighting the selected plan while calming faded indicators for non-selected plans.
The deposit information remains separate, emphasizing its refundable nature and clarifying that it is not included in the total accommodation cost.
