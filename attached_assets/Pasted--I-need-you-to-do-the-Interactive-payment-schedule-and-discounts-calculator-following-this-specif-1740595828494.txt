

I need you to do the Interactive payment schedule and discounts calculator following this specific prepayment discount logic:

## Requirements:

1. Create a function that takes these inputs:
   - Total number of nights
   - 1-day/7-days/30-days plans
   - Plan rate for the plan period
   - Standard per night cost for each plan
   - Start date

2. Calculate the initial payment schedule with:
   - First plan period prepaid at regular plan rate
   - Remaining full and partial periods  shown as upcoming payments
   - "+" and "-" buttons to add/remove prepaid periods

3. Progressive discount logic:
   - First prepaid period: regular price 
   - Second prepaid period: 5% discount 
   - Third prepaid period: 10% discount 
   - Fourth prepaid period: 15% discount 
   - Fifth prepaid period and onwards: 20 % discount
   - Any partial periods get the discount at the level they would be if it was full period

4. Each time a user clicks "+":
   - Move the next unpaid period to the prepaid section
   - Apply the appropriate discount percentage
   - Recalculate total prepaid amount
   - Update the remaining payment schedule
   - Update the discount percentage for the next potential prepayment

5. Display these sections:
   - "To be prepaid at booking" showing total prepaid amount with date ranges
   - Button section with discount information for next potential prepayment
   - "Upcoming future payments" showing remaining unpaid periods with amounts and dates

6. The UI should update dynamically when users click "+" or "-" buttons

7. Include a final state showing when all periods are prepaid

## Example Flow:

For a 23-night stay starting May 1st:
- Initial state: 1 week prepaid ($420), option to prepay next week at 5% off
- After clicking "+": 2 weeks prepaid ($819), option to prepay next week at 10% off
- After clicking "+" again: 3 weeks prepaid ($1,197), option to prepay remaining 2 days at 15% off
- After clicking "+" again: All prepaid ($1,299), no further payments needed

Please provide working code with HTML/CSS for the interface, and JavaScript for the calculation logic and event handling.
